## Authored interpretation

Native landscape iPhone SpriteKit fighter, **Hollow Afterdark**, with two original
duelists: **Ren / Riftblade** (white hair, black long coat, crimson blade) and
**Aya / Moonwire** (violet hair, ivory coat, cyan saber). Both have the same
competitive move rules; silhouettes and effect colors differ. Authored vector
character rigs, moonlit elevated station plaza, depth-layered skyline, railings,
rain and reflected light provide the urban supernatural direction without
copying character art.

The **Undertow** row uses the observed opposing diamonds and circular cycle.
Every 12 seconds the advantage leader receives **ASCEND** (10% damage) and may
spend it on **SHIFT** to cancel recovery and gain meter. This period, exact frame
data, damage, simplified touch inputs and new terminology are design choices.
Walking into danger builds advantage; retreat drains it. Shields steal advantage
but drain meter and lose to throws. Light → heavy → special cancels, jump attacks,
projectiles, hit sparks, block stun, throw break, air movement, pushback and best-of-three
rounds make positioning and resource timing consequential.
