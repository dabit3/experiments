Reference: **Guilty Gear Xrd -SIGN- (2014)**. Rift Requiem is an original iPhone
weapon fighter inspired by the reference, not an authorized port.

of 2014 exact mechanics. The official product page confirms tutorials, player
rooms and varied playable silhouettes. Its conflicting "Playable Online: No"
metadata was not used as a behavior specification.

## Authored interpretation

Rook is a broad red-coated engine executioner with an enormous toothed cleaver,
gold mechanical shoulder and asymmetrical gauntlet. Vesper is a narrow ivory
cathedral duelist with a swept hair crest, long split coat and crescent scythe.
Both are original silhouettes, not copies of Sol or Ky. The Cathedral Engine
background was generated specifically for this app and is rendered behind the
fighters. All fighter shapes, poses, particles, UI ornaments and audio are authored
in source. No ROMs or extracted/proprietary assets are shipped.

Acceptance inventory: guest lobby/room create/join; two real peers; ground dash;
one air dash per jump; jump and air attacks; directional guard/chip;
light/heavy recovery and hitstun; different weapons and projectile specials;
meter gain; yellow/red/purple cancel and slowdown; best-of-three rounds;
results/rematch; reconnect pause; responsive landscape HUD; audio and hit effects.

## Evidence boundaries

Original arcade software is unavailable. Frame data, balance, original 3D camera,
exact character animation, assets, full roster, story, online ranking and all
advanced Xrd systems are not reproduced. Native skeletal 2D vector animation is
used in place of Xrd's authored 3D cel animation. No literal or normalized pixel
parity is claimed. The clone-this research/inventory/evidence workflow applies;
its zero-pixel gate cannot pass for this authorized original approximation.
