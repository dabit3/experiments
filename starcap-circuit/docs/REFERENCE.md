## Applied design

Native landscape iPhone with a real 3D chase camera. Rubber tires, glossy capsule
karts, three rounded animal/astronaut silhouettes: Pip (coral space squirrel),
Mochi (mint rabbit), and Volt (gold robot). Outlined condensed rounded typography,
top-center item roulette, enormous rank/lap, countdown, miniature circuit map,
electric drift sparks, and trophy/confetti result presentation.

**Sunpetal Speedway** uses cyan water, grassy islands, striped kerbs, candy-colored
turrets, puffy trees, pennants and balloon arches. **Neon Bonbon** uses purple
asphalt, pink trees, glowing lanterns, golden rails and a dusk palette, with a
different tighter double-bend course. Both have sequential checkpoints and two laps.

## Observed versus inferred

Visual composition above is directly observed. Multiplayer modes and item breadth
are documented. Steering forces, drift thresholds, item targeting, physics,
race lengths and network protocol are original implementations inferred from
arcade racing conventions, not measured against an accessible cabinet.

The original software/cabinet was not available for interactive testing. No literal
pixel parity, handling parity, exhaustive clone-this gate, licensed-character
equivalence, fusion mode, gliding, underwater transformations, camera portraits,
40-round progression or 90-item equivalence is claimed. Scope is the assigned
two-track versus kart racer with original art, music, and real room networking.
